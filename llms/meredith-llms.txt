# People Inc (formerly Dotdash Meredith)

> America's largest digital and print publisher, an IAC operating company, running 40+ consumer
> brands including PEOPLE, Better Homes & Gardens, Allrecipes, Investopedia, Verywell, Food & Wine,
> Southern Living, Real Simple, Travel + Leisure, EatingWell, Serious Eats and Entertainment Weekly.
> People Inc publishes NO developer API. Its machine-readable surface is discovery metadata only:
> robots.txt, XML sitemaps, a Google News sitemap, and an RFC 9116 security.txt. Editorial content
> is closed to AI crawlers by both robots.txt and an edge rule that answers HTTP 402 Payment
> Required with a licensing contact.
>
> GENERATED by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-08-12 from this repo's artifacts and
> live probes. People Inc does not publish an llms.txt of its own — /llms.txt was probed on
> www.people.inc, people.com, www.allrecipes.com, www.investopedia.com, www.bhg.com and www.iac.com
> and returned 403 everywhere except www.iac.com, which returns an HTML soft-200 that is not a
> document. This file is API Evangelist's description of People Inc, not People Inc's own.

## Before you crawl — read this first

- People Inc denies AI crawlers in robots.txt across the estate: https://people.com/robots.txt
- Requests identifying as an AI crawler receive HTTP 402 Payment Required, not 403.
- The 402 body reads: "Payment Required - If you wish to license content from People Inc, please
  contact contentlicensing@people.inc"
- Content licensing: contentlicensing@people.inc
- Brand terms of service: https://www.people.inc/brands-termsofservice
- The robots.txt comment block asserts an EU DSM Article 4 text-and-data-mining reservation and
  expressly prohibits training, fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, and dataset creation.
- Sponsored/branded content paths (`*/presented/`, `*/integrated/`) are explicitly ALLOWED to the
  same AI crawlers that are otherwise denied.

## What is actually machine-readable

- [robots.txt (people.com)](https://people.com/robots.txt): RFC 9309, 78 user-agent directives, 200 text/plain
- [robots.txt (corporate)](https://www.people.inc/robots.txt): separate policy for www.people.inc, 200 text/plain
- [Sitemap index (people.com)](https://people.com/sitemap.xml): sitemaps.org 0.9, 26 child sitemaps, 200 text/xml
- [Google News sitemap (people.com)](https://people.com/google-news-sitemap.xml): 200 text/xml
- [Sitemap index (Allrecipes)](https://www.allrecipes.com/sitemap.xml): 4 child sitemaps, 200 text/xml
- [Sitemap (corporate)](https://www.people.inc/sitemap.xml): 34 URLs, 200 application/xml
- [security.txt](https://people.com/.well-known/security.txt): RFC 9116, 200 text/plain, private HackerOne program

Everything else — article pages, RSS feeds, and every /.well-known/ path other than security.txt —
returns HTTP 403 to machine clients regardless of user-agent.

## What does not exist

- No OpenAPI or Swagger document. Probed /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /api-docs,
  /docs and /v1/openapi.json across six hosts.
- No GraphQL endpoint. No api.* or developer.* subdomain resolves for people.inc or people.com.
- No MCP server. No A2A agent card at /.well-known/agent-card.json or /.well-known/agent.json.
- No SDKs or client libraries in any package registry. GitHub org MeredithCorp holds one repo, a
  2021 fork of bbc/sqs-consumer. People-INC holds none.
- No status page, no SLA, no deprecation policy, no trust centre, no published certifications.
